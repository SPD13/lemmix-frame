Installer

1 Connect to your Steam Frame

On the Frame, open Settings > System and switch Developer Mode on. Keep the headset on so it stays awake.

    2 Install or update Lemmix

    On the Frame- Latest version-
    Remove Lemmix and its library entry from the Frame? The levels, styles, settings and replays it downloaded are kept, unless you tick the box.

    Lemmix is running on the Frame: quit it first, then select Connect again.

# Boot splash

`splash.png` is Godot's boot splash (`application/boot_splash/image`), shown while the app starts;
`subtitle.png` is the "STEAM FRAME EDITION" line the lobby draws under the NeoLemmix logo. Both
are made by `tools/art/make-art.sh` (with the Steam library art in `steam/library/`) from a
NeoLemmix release's menu graphics (the newer releases': `gfx/menu/logo.png`, the dirt
`gfx/menu/background.png`, `gfx/menu/sign_group.png`).

The NeoLemmix logo and background are NeoLemmix's artwork; per NeoLemmix's licence their creators
keep all rights to them ("they may be downloaded, used and copied only for the purpose of running
NeoLemmix"). They are bundled here at the project owner's decision (3 Oct 2026).
